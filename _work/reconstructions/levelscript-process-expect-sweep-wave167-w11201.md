## Method

A harness (`/dev/shm/w11201/sweep.py`) parses every `if (...)` in the
`process()` body (lines 605-5305), splits each condition into its top-level
`&&`/`||` operands, and wraps one operand (or a whole single-operand
condition, or a ternary condition) in `__builtin_expect(..., e)`, one cell at
a time; it rebuilds the full `.so` (`ninja link_so`, NDK r18b clang 7.0.2,
-Oz), and measures raw diff / function size / `.rel.plt` / `.ARM.extab`
after every candidate.  A full cycle is ~1.5 s.  Landed shapes are re-applied
with `apply3.py` (waiver comment placed at the end of the line, or on the
`__builtin_expect` line with a newline for multi-line operands).

Three sweeps were run:

1. **Operand sweep (498 cells)** — every top-level `&&`/`||` operand of every
   multi-operand `if` (all indices, both e=0 and e=1), plus every ternary
   condition.  The wave-166 worker had only tested the *last* operand cold.
   6 wins landed.
2. **Full-if sweep (772 cells)** — every `if` condition (single- and
   multi-operand) with e=0 and e=1, re-run on the post-commit-1 base.  4 wins
   landed.
3. **Ternary re-sweep (38 cells)** — the 12 cells the first parser missed
   (declaration initialisers such as `int signX = (...) ? ...`) plus all
   other ternaries.  All neutral except `target == 4` (worse); none landed.
4. **Operand re-sweep (486 cells)** — the same operand/ternary sweep re-run
   on the post-commit-2 base (54,032) to catch cells that only win after the
   layout moved.  One win landed (commit 3).
5. **Nested-group sweep (4 cells)** — parenthesised `&&`/`||` subexpressions
   inside larger conditions (`L1558`, `L4881`).  All worse or size-changing;
   none landed.

### Interactions

Greedy combination on each base: the 6 operand/ternary wins are additive
(54,095 -> 54,063).  On the new base the 4 full-if wins are additive
(54,063 -> 54,032).  Two cells that win alone do **not** combine:
`L5106:1` (54061 alone) and `L5132:0` (54060 alone) make 54,033 / 54,036 when
added to the landed 4; together with `L3111:1` they give 54,030 but at
function size 57,236 (gate-fatal, no size-compensating companion found in
time).  `L3111:1` alone on the landed 4 is 54,037 / size 57,236.
